# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct & Scientific Integrity Pledge

## Our Pledge
We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in the **OmniResearch** community a harassment-free and intellectually rigorous experience for everyone, regardless of academic discipline, background, level of experience, or personal identity.

## Scientific & Academic Ethics
As a project dedicated to the advancement of open, rigorous, and reproducible science:
1. **Honesty & Transparency**: We do not promote, facilitate, or condone data fabrication, p-hacking, or intentional misrepresentation of empirical findings.
2. **Open Source & Open Science**: We champion open methodologies, reproducible pipelines, and FAIR data practices.
3. **Respectful Peer Review**: Critique should focus on methodologies, empirical rigor, and logic, remaining constructive and respectful at all times.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or unethical behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at [https://github.com/krsdntr/OmniResearch/issues](https://github.com/krsdntr/OmniResearch/issues).
